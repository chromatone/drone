# Chromatone Drone

![Color drone app](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chromatone/drone/refs/heads/main/public/screen.png)

## Rich harmonic soundscape synthesizer for music practice, chanting and meditation

You choose the pitch and the app plays 3 octaves and 3 fifths intervals for it. The sound is generated with [sawtooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawtooth_wave) oscillator synth and this gives the incredible amount of harmonic material to play with. Consider it as an electronic version of [indian tanpura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanpura) or simply the [Shruti box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shruti_box).

### How to use the drone app

1. Swipe the top section to choose your root pitch — the central tone everything else builds around.
2. Tap any voice cell to toggle it on. Each voice is an independent instrument.
3. Each voice has 4 control quadrants — drag to shape its sound:
   - **VOL/PAN** — volume and stereo position
   - **LP/Q** — filter brightness and resonance
   - **AF** — auto-filter rate and depth
   - **CHO** — chorus modulation depth
4. Use the master volume knob in the top bar for overall level.
5. Let the voices breathe — each has its own subtle LFO movement.
6. Sing, tune, or meditate with the evolving harmonic landscape.
